About me

I hold an M.Sc. in Geography and am now pursuing an M.Tech in Geoinformatics. My research centres on multi-hazard risk assessment and socio-economic vulnerability, using GIS, remote sensing and Python to process complex geospatial datasets.

I like work that starts in the field. I have collected about 1,360 assets in a panchayat using participatory mapping, run transect walks and interviews, and then taken that data back to QGIS and ArcGIS Pro to build land use, flood and landslide vulnerability maps.

I'm looking for a team where spatial analysis supports real decisions on disaster preparedness and natural resource management.
